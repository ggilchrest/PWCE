Lifestream permission recovery

What changed

Lifestream now looks up a pending original permission when its reply was lost. It keeps the original decision after restarting its storage connection and does not send another permission request.

Why it matters

Recovery preserves the original expiry. Reading an old decision does not permit a new command: current host approval and dispatch checks still apply. A request that PWCE cannot find remains uncertain.

What to test

  • Missing permission reply checks — verify Lifestream restart, concurrent recovery, unchanged target calls and refusal to dispatch expired permission.

Where to read more

Recover a missing action-permission reply.

Screenshots updated

None; Studio controls and layout are unchanged.

Known issues / follow-ups

Full adapter composition, authority-context restoration and saved runtime activation remain separate work. These synthetic checks do not claim physical-device or Human acceptance.
