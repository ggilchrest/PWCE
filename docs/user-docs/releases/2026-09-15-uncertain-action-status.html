Check uncertain action results

What changed

Checking an uncertain action can now confirm its result through the original target, without sending the action again. The original reply stays in the history. Slow, malformed or late replies cannot erase a confirmed result.

Home Assistant confirmation now needs a fresh reading from the correct site, source and device. An old matching brightness value is not enough.

Why it matters

You can check what happened after an interruption without risking an accidental repeat or mistaking an old reading for success.

What to test

  • Inspect local context QA — focus on the uncertain-action criteria and synthetic regression check.

Where to read more

Screenshots updated

No screen layout changed. Existing screenshots remain applicable.

Known issues / follow-ups

Full Assistant integration remains in progress. Historical actions without an original target binding remain uncertain. These checks do not establish live-device or Human acceptance.
